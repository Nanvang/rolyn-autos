COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sourcing, buying, and shipping your car with Raleigh Auto.

1. Where does Raleigh Auto source its vehicles?

Raleigh Auto sources vehicles directly from Canada. We work with licensed Canadian car dealerships, reputable wholesale auction networks, and verified private inventories primarily across Ontario, Quebec, Alberta, and British Columbia. This gives you direct access to clean, well-maintained Canadian vehicles.

2. Can I request a vehicle that is not listed on the website?

Yes, absolutely! The vehicles displayed on our website are sample representations of available inventory. If you have a specific make, model, trim, color, or budget in mind that is not featured, simply fill out our Vehicle Sourcing Request form or contact us directly via WhatsApp. Our team in Canada will search the market specifically for your requested car.

3. Can Raleigh Auto purchase a vehicle on my behalf?

Yes. Assisting customers with the purchasing process is one of our primary core services. Once you review and approve a vehicle quotation, we coordinate the purchase with the Canadian dealer or seller, handle ownership transfer paperwork, and ensure all purchase documents are legitimately completed in your name or as agreed.

4. Does Raleigh Auto ship vehicles directly to Ghana?

Yes. We arrange and coordinate international ocean shipping from Canadian ports (such as Montreal or Halifax) directly to Ghana’s primary commercial ports: Tema Port and Takoradi Port. We handle export documentation in Canada and coordinate with reputable ocean shipping lines.

5. How long does shipping from Canada to Ghana take?

Shipping duration depends on the departure port in Canada, vessel availability, shipping schedules, and the chosen shipping method (containerized or Roll-on/Roll-off). On average, ocean transit takes approximately 4 to 8 weeks after container loading and vessel departure. We provide estimated timelines with each quotation and keep you informed throughout the voyage.

6. What payment methods are accepted?

We accommodate several convenient and secure payment channels, including Ghanaian Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), direct bank transfers into approved Ghanaian commercial bank accounts, and international bank wire transfers (SWIFT/TT in CAD or USD). Payment details are provided on official invoices after agreement.

7. Do I need to travel to Canada to purchase a vehicle?

No, not at all! The entire purpose of Raleigh Auto is to eliminate the need for you to travel to Canada. You can sit comfortably in Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi, or anywhere in Ghana while our team handles vehicle sourcing, physical inspection, negotiations, purchase, export, and international shipping on your behalf.

8. Are the listed vehicles brand new or used?

We source both high-quality pre-owned vehicles (including Certified Pre-Owned models from authorized dealerships) and brand-new vehicles upon request. The majority of imported vehicles are clean, late-model used cars that offer excellent value, inspected for structural integrity and documented history.

9. Are customs duties and other importation charges included in the vehicle price?

Vehicle quotations typically cover the vehicle purchase cost and international ocean freight to Ghana. Ghanaian customs duties, port handling fees, and statutory statutory levies (determined by the Ghana Revenue Authority - GRA) are assessed individually upon arrival based on vehicle age, engine capacity, and current customs tariffs. We provide clear guidance and estimated duty calculations upon request.

10. How can I track or receive updates about my vehicle?

We provide continuous, personalized communication through WhatsApp and email. You will receive photo and video documentation of your vehicle upon purchase, inspection reports, container loading confirmation, and the official shipping line Bill of Lading with tracking details once the vessel departs Canada.
